FindingDoctor Account Deletion

Request deletion of your FindingDoctor account and associated personal information.

If you have a FindingDoctor account and want it deleted, use the email link below to submit a deletion request. This page is available even if you have uninstalled the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Select the “Request account deletion” link below.
  2. Send the message from the email address associated with your FindingDoctor account where possible.
  3. Include your account email address and a short request to delete your FindingDoctor account. Do not include your password, verification codes, or unnecessary sensitive information.
  4. We may contact you to verify that you control the account before processing the request.

Request account deletion

What happens next

After verifying the request, FindingDoctor will process deletion of the account and associated personal information that we control, unless specific information must be retained for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or other lawful reasons. Relevant third-party service providers will be addressed as applicable. Some content already shared with other users, security records and residual backup copies may not disappear immediately from every location.

We do not state a fixed completion period on this page. We will handle requests within the time required by applicable law and our operational process.
