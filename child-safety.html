FindingDentist Child Safety Standards

Standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)

Effective date: 19 October 2026

FindingDentist is a professional community intended for adult dental professionals in India. The service is not designed for children, and users are expected to be adults. These Child Safety Standards apply to the FindingDentist app and any community content or interactions made available through it.

Zero tolerance. FindingDentist prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), and any content or conduct that sexualises, exploits, grooms, abuses, endangers, or facilitates harm to a person under 18.

1. Prohibited content and conduct

Users must not create, upload, post, request, share, promote, distribute, store, or link to content or engage in conduct that involves or facilitates:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM), including real, altered, synthetic, or computer-generated visual depictions of a minor involved in sexually explicit conduct.
  • Sexualisation of minors, sexual comments or solicitations directed at a minor, or content that encourages sexual activity involving a minor.
  • Grooming, coercion, sextortion, sexual exploitation, trafficking, or attempts to arrange sexual access to a minor.
  • Requests for, offers to exchange, or directions to obtain CSAM or other exploitative material involving children.
  • Threats, encouragement, instructions, or other assistance that facilitates child sexual abuse or exploitation.

Attempts to evade this policy through coded language, external links, private communications, altered material, or other methods are also prohibited.

2. Reporting a concern

Anyone who encounters suspected CSAE or CSAM connected with FindingDentist should report it promptly to our child-safety contact at abhishekbaliya4@gmail.com, using the subject line “FindingDentist Child Safety Report”. Include a description of the concern and relevant context, such as the username, post or page location, and approximate date, if known. Do not download, forward, attach, or redistribute suspected CSAM to make a report.

If a child appears to be in immediate danger, contact local law enforcement or emergency services. Do not rely solely on an email to FindingDentist in an emergency.

3. Review and enforcement

FindingDentist treats reports of suspected CSAE seriously. Where we become aware of content or conduct that may violate these standards, we will assess the concern and take appropriate action based on the circumstances and applicable law. Actions may include removing or restricting access to content, disabling relevant functionality, suspending or terminating accounts, preserving relevant information where lawful and necessary, and preventing repeat violations.

We may take action without prior notice when necessary to protect a child, comply with law, or prevent further harm. We may also cooperate with lawful investigations and provide relevant information to appropriate authorities when legally required or permitted.

4. Child sexual abuse material and legal reporting

FindingDentist prohibits CSAM and will take appropriate action when we obtain actual knowledge of CSAM on or connected with the service, including removing or disabling access to it as appropriate and handling relevant evidence lawfully. We will report confirmed CSAM to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the appropriate regional authority, as applicable and required by law, and cooperate with relevant child-safety and law-enforcement authorities.

We will not knowingly permit FindingDentist to be used to create, store, request, or distribute CSAM or to facilitate child sexual abuse or exploitation.

6. User responsibilities

Users must comply with these standards, applicable law, and the FindingDentist community rules. Users must not attempt to investigate suspected CSAM by copying, saving, or circulating it. Reports should be made promptly and in good faith, with only the information needed to describe the concern.

7. Updates

We may update these standards as the FindingDentist service, applicable laws, or child-safety requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
