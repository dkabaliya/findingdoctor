FindingDoctor Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FindingDoctor (“FindingDoctor”, “we”, “us” or “our”) handles information when you use the FindingDoctor mobile application and related services (the “Service”). FindingDoctor is intended to support a professional community for dentists; it is not intended to be a doctor–patient consultation or emergency-care service.

Privacy contact: abhishekbaliya4@gmail.com

Important: This policy is intended to describe the Service as configured and used. The app’s owner should keep it updated to reflect actual app behaviour, enabled Firebase products, data flows, retention practices and deletion functionality. Do not treat this policy as confirmation that a feature is implemented unless it has been tested.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use and the information you submit, the Service may process:

  • Account and contact information: name, email address, mobile number and account identifiers.
  • Professional and registration information: whether you hold a BDS or MDS qualification, dental council details, registration number, and information submitted to establish eligibility as a dental professional.
  • Verification information: information you provide for credential review and the resulting verification status, such as pending, verified or rejected. We may use professional-register information to assess credentials where available and appropriate.
  • Community content and activity: posts, comments, likes, replies, and related interaction or moderation records you create or submit.
  • Technical and service information: information processed by the app, its authentication provider or hosting and security services to operate, protect, troubleshoot and maintain the Service. The exact information depends on the services and settings enabled.
  • Messages to us: the details you include when contacting support or making a privacy or deletion request.

Please do not upload patient-identifiable information, patient records, sensitive clinical details or another person’s personal information to community areas unless the feature expressly permits it and you have a lawful basis and any required permissions.

2. How we use information

Information may be used to create and maintain accounts; authenticate users; assess whether applicants meet the professional eligibility criteria; operate the dentist community; display community content and interactions; respond to enquiries and deletion requests; prevent abuse, fraud or security incidents; enforce community rules; troubleshoot and improve the Service; and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Professional verification

FindingDoctor is designed for qualified dentists, initially focusing on BDS/MDS professionals. Information such as qualifications, council details and registration numbers may be reviewed to determine access or verification status. Submission of registration details does not itself guarantee verification. Access decisions may require manual review or checking against relevant professional records.

4. Community content and visibility

Content you publish in community features, including posts and comments, and associated interactions such as likes, may be visible to other users according to the app’s feature design. Do not include anything you would not be comfortable sharing with the relevant community audience. Content may remain visible to others, or in backups or moderation records, for a limited period after you edit, remove or request deletion, where technically necessary or legally permitted. We do not promise that other users will not copy or retain content they were able to see.

5. Firebase and other service providers

The app is built using Firebase-related services for app infrastructure and authentication. Depending on the configuration, Google or Firebase may process account identifiers, authentication and security information, and data stored or transmitted through services enabled in the app. Their processing is also subject to the applicable provider terms and privacy documentation.

We may rely on hosting, infrastructure, security, professional-register or other service providers as needed to deliver the Service. Providers should receive only information reasonably needed for their functions and may process it under their own terms or contractual obligations. The app owner should update this section if analytics, crash reporting, messaging, storage or other third-party SDKs are enabled.

6. When information may be shared

We do not describe account or professional information as being sold. Information may be shared:

  • with other FindingDoctor users when you submit content or when account/professional details are displayed as part of community or verification features;
  • with service providers supporting authentication, hosting, security, maintenance or operation of the Service;
  • when reasonably necessary to protect users, investigate abuse, enforce rules, respond to legal process or protect rights and safety; or
  • with your direction or consent, where applicable.

7. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain professional verification and security, administer community activity, resolve disputes and comply with legal obligations. Retention periods can vary by data type and purpose. When information is no longer needed, it should be deleted or de-identified where feasible, subject to lawful retention requirements, security logs and backup cycles.

FindingDoctor should document and follow actual retention schedules for Firebase Authentication accounts, database records, uploaded material, community posts, moderation records and backups. This policy does not assert a fixed deletion deadline that has not been technically verified.

8. Account deletion and privacy requests

You can request deletion of your FindingDoctor account and associated personal information by emailing abhishekbaliya4@gmail.com with the subject “FindingDoctor account deletion”. Please send the request from the email address associated with the account where possible and do not send passwords or one-time codes.

We may ask for reasonable information to verify that the request comes from the account holder. After verification, we will process the request and delete or de-identify personal information that we control unless retention is required or permitted by applicable law, security requirements, dispute resolution or legitimate recordkeeping needs. Content already shared with other users, records retained lawfully, and residual copies in backup systems may not disappear immediately from every location.

For the app owner: Google Play requires an account-deletion pathway within the app and a working external web resource for deletion requests when users can create accounts. An email contact by itself should not be considered a substitute for implementing and testing all applicable in-app deletion requirements. Add or link the actual deletion-request webpage when it is available.

9. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the Service to help protect information, but no storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your account credentials and notify us if you suspect unauthorised access. The app owner should ensure that database access rules, administrative permissions, authentication and credential-review records are appropriately restricted and tested.

10. Children

FindingDoctor is a professional service intended for qualified adult dental professionals. It is not designed for children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children through the Service.

11. Your choices and applicable rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, or to raise an objection or complaint. To make a request, contact us using the email below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

12. International processing

Service providers may process information on infrastructure located in countries other than your own. Where applicable, we will take steps required by law for such processing.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Service, its providers or legal requirements change. We will publish the updated version on this page and revise the effective date. Please review this page periodically.
